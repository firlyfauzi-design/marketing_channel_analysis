# Marketing Channel Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance across different channels, campaign types, advertising spend levels, and customer segments.

The goal is to identify the factors associated with customer conversions and provide actionable recommendations to improve marketing effectiveness.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 8,000 customer records with information related to marketing campaigns, customer demographics, advertising spend, and conversion outcomes.

Data quality checks were performed before analysis:
- Missing Values: 0
- Duplicate Records: 0

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Business Questions

1. Which marketing channel has the highest conversion rate?
2. Which campaign type generates the highest conversion rate?
3. Does higher advertising spend lead to more conversions?
4. Which customer demographic group has the highest conversion rate?
5. Which marketing channel is the most cost-efficient?

## Analysis Process

1. Data Understanding
2. Data Quality Checking
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
4. Marketing Channel Analysis
5. Customer Segment Analysis
6. Data Visualization
7. Business Insight Generation

## Key Insights

- Referral achieved the highest conversion rate among the analyzed marketing channels.
- PPC also demonstrated strong conversion performance.
- Conversion rates were relatively similar across marketing channels.
- Higher advertising spend did not necessarily result in higher conversion performance.
- Marketing effectiveness should therefore be evaluated using both conversion performance and advertising costs.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize and further evaluate referral marketing strategies due to their strong conversion performance.
- Continue optimizing PPC campaigns while monitoring advertising costs.
- Avoid increasing advertising budgets based solely on spend; evaluate conversion efficiency before reallocating budget.
- Use customer segmentation to improve campaign targeting.
- Conduct further testing, such as A/B testing, to identify campaign strategies that improve conversion performance.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Referral and PPC are among the strongest-performing marketing channels based on conversion rate. However, differences between channels are relatively small, and higher advertising spend does not automatically lead to better conversion outcomes.

Marketing decisions should therefore consider both conversion performance and cost efficiency. Future analysis could incorporate additional metrics such as revenue, ROI, and customer lifetime value to provide a more comprehensive assessment of marketing performance.
